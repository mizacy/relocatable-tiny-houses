Building, zoning, and implementation guidance for communities seeking clear pathways for small, safe homes.

Model Regulatory Framework for Relocatable Tiny Houses

Relocatable tiny houses are small homes built on an integral chassis and designed to be moved and installed on a site for residential occupancy. Their ability to be relocated creates questions about construction, placement, inspections, and how the home connects to its site.

This framework brings together proposed building code language, model zoning provisions, and practical implementation guidance to help jurisdictions address those questions. The goal is to provide a useful starting point that communities can evaluate and adapt to their local needs, applicable laws, and regulatory authority.

The work builds on the effort to recognize relocatable tiny houses within the International Residential Code and on lessons from communities developing their own approval pathways.

What the framework includes

  • Understanding the housing

    Background on residential uses, separate ownership of the home and land, and the relationship between the dwelling, the prepared site, and its installation.

  • Building code language

    Proposed provisions for chassis systems, structural connections, site support and anchorage, and approval pathways for new and existing homes.

  • Model zoning provisions

    Tools for addressing where these homes may be permitted, site requirements, and local policy choices about their use within communities.

  • Implementation guidance

    Practical considerations for permitting, inspections, documentation, utilities, departmental coordination, and occupancy approval.

Keeping the pathway accessible

A useful residential approval process should accommodate owner-builders, small local builders, community efforts, and professionally manufactured homes. The framework aims to preserve these options while establishing clear ways to demonstrate compliance with applicable safety requirements.

Communities retain authority over local land-use decisions. The objective is to make the requirements and approval process understandable, predictable, and workable for residents and officials alike.

Review and community participation

The framework is currently undergoing technical and implementation review. Some provisions remain under development.

We welcome inquiries from building officials, planners, jurisdiction staff, and technical and housing professionals interested in evaluating the framework or exploring its application in their communities.

To discuss participating in the review, please send a brief introduction with your organization or jurisdiction, your area of interest, and how you anticipate using the framework. Working drafts are shared directly as part of this review process.

Residents interested in bringing this approach to their community are also welcome to reach out, particularly to connect the project with interested local officials.
